ClipBot Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

What ClipBot is

ClipBot is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload video clips to the owner's own YouTube channel. It has no other users.

Google data it uses

  • ClipBot asks for one Google permission: uploading videos to YouTube (youtube.upload).
  • It uses that permission only to upload clips and set their titles, descriptions, tags and publish times.
  • It does not read, collect, or store any other Google account data.

Storage and sharing

  • The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account permissions.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the project's GitHub page.
